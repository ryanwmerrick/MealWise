# MealWise local walkthrough

1. Tap **Get started**, review the household, days and budget, then tap **Build my plan**. Week shows the budget, scheduled meals and ingredient map.
2. Open a recipe or preview a swap to see how the complete grocery cart changes. In **Groceries**, start shopping, check purchased packages and enter sample receipt prices as needed.
3. Open **Today**, complete cooking and choose **Yes, save to Favorites** on the cooked meal. Open **Plan settings → Weekly nutrition** to see vegetable, protein and whole-grain counts for the scheduled meals.
4. In **Pantry**, open **Wrap up this cycle**, check the stock you want to carry forward and confirm. Relaunch MealWise. Open **Plan next week → Favorites and next-plan pins**, choose the saved recipe and pin it to a slot.
5. Review next-week setup, build the plan and confirm that the pinned recipe and applicable pantry stock appear in the preview. Accept the plan. Open **Cycle history** to see the previous cycle and its recorded receipt.

**Start over** clears the active plan and setup answers while keeping pantry, preferences and history. Use **Saved data → Erase all local data** from Welcome to clear all demo records and begin with defaults. Plan settings also offers this action while a plan is active.

Use sample data in the walkthrough. The bundled catalog’s synthetic offers are valid through December 13, 2026 UTC. New estimates require current fixture dates. Existing saved plans and historical records keep their original recipe and price snapshots.
