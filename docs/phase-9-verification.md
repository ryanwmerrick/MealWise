# Phase 9 — wrap-up, pantry and the next cycle

Wrap-up separates the original all-meals forecast, recorded stock remaining after confirmed cooking, and the user's confirmed carryover. Confirmation is source-specific: purchased stock and previously reserved pantry lots keep their own quantities/storage/dates. Unchecked sources are not silently added to inventory. A closed cycle preserves purchases, cooked recipes/portions, incomplete receipt information, and the exact carryover confirmation.

Closing is one repository transaction. It releases/reconciles the old reserved lots, creates deterministic carryover lot IDs, stores immutable history, and closes the active cycle. Duplicate confirmation cannot create more stock. Closed cycles reject further shopping, cooking and plan edits; history and receipts remain readable. Reset archives remain distinct from completed cycles.

Pantry add/edit/remove uses normalized amounts, optional user-entered storage/use-by dates, explicit availability, and revision checks. Unknown dates remain uncertain. Expired or unconfirmed stock is excluded from planning. Editing/removing stock updates remaining reservations and the active cart together; cooked source debits remain immutable. A physical shortage may leave a visible over-budget review state; ordinary plan acceptance still requires a feasible budget.

Planning previews reserve nothing. Acceptance rechecks source identity, revisions, free quantities and expiry eligibility, then reserves stock atomically with the new plan/profile. Cooking records exactly which pantry/purchase pools supplied a meal, reducing physical pantry quantity and its reservation together. Existing records without source debits remain purchase-pool records for compatibility.

Next-week setup prefills the prior choices and advances dates by complete calendar weeks. Interrupted future drafts restore their answers. Date controls deduplicate local days while retaining historical selected-date identities. A typed destination carries the prepared catalog directly, avoiding an empty navigation destination from separate optional state.

Savings price the same selected meals with and without confirmed stock. Small credits that leave the same whole-package count produce zero savings. Receipt observations are optional and require explicit selection before updating future estimates; non-divisible package averages round up to a cent. Exact historical receipt totals and the original catalog validity remain unchanged.
